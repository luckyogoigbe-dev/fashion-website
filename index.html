OUR STORY

Made to Be Worn, Not Just Owned

VELOURA was founded on a simple idea: clothing should feel as good as it looks. Every piece is designed in-house and produced in small, considered runs — favoring quality and craftsmanship over fast fashion's churn.

From sourcing responsibly-milled fabric to finishing every seam by hand, we treat each garment as something meant to last — in your wardrobe, not a landfill.
